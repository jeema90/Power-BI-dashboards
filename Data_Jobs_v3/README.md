# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard


![Dashboard page 1](/images/Screenshot%202026-06-04%20123146.png)

---------------------
## 📌 Overview

The **Sales Analysis Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of sales performance across teams, regions, geographies, and sales representatives. It enables users to monitor key business metrics, identify top performers, analyze regional contributions, and track monthly sales and profit trends through interactive visualizations.

---

## 🚀 Dashboard Features

### 💰 KPI Cards

The dashboard highlights two key performance indicators:

- 💵 **Total Sales Amount:** $30M
- 📈 **Total Profit:** $23M

These gauge visuals provide a quick snapshot of overall business performance against target values.

---

### 👨‍💼 Sales Person Performance

A detailed table displaying:

- 🖼️ Salesperson profile image
- 👤 Salesperson name
- 💰 Total sales amount generated

This visual helps identify top-performing sales representatives and compare individual contributions.

---

### 👥 Amount by Team

A horizontal bar chart comparing sales amounts across different teams:

- 🔹 Apex
- 🔹 Nexus
- 🔹 Catalyst
- 🔹 Special Team

This visual helps evaluate team-level performance and productivity.

---

### 🌍 Amount by Geography

A pie chart showing sales distribution across countries:

- 🇺🇸 USA
- 🇮🇳 India
- 🇬🇧 UK
- 🇳🇿 New Zealand
- 🇨🇦 Canada
- 🇦🇺 Australia

This provides insights into geographic revenue contributions.

---

### 🏆 Amount by Sales Person

A donut chart displaying the percentage contribution of each salesperson to overall sales.

Useful for quickly identifying the highest contributors.

---

### 🗺️ Amount by Region

A column chart comparing sales performance across major regions:

- 🌏 APAC
- 🌎 Americas
- 🌍 Europe

This visual helps analyze regional sales strengths and opportunities.

---

### 📅 Amount and Profit by Month

A line chart tracking monthly trends for:

- 💵 Sales Amount
- 📈 Profit

This visualization helps identify:

- 📊 Seasonal patterns
- 📈 Growth trends
- 🔍 High and low performing months

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for:

### 📅 Date Filter
Allows users to analyze sales data for specific time periods.

### 👥 Team Filter
Enables users to focus on performance metrics for selected teams.

### 🔄 Clear All Slicers
A dedicated button to reset all active filters and return the dashboard to its default state.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

Using this dashboard, users can:

- ✅ Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
- 🏅 Identify top-performing sales representatives.
- 👥 Compare team productivity.
- 🌎 Analyze regional and geographic sales distribution.
- 📈 Track monthly business trends.
- 🎯 Perform interactive filtering for deeper analysis.
