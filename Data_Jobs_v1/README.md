# Data Jobs Dashboard with Power Bi

![Dashboard page 1](/images/Screenshot%202026-05-27%20121218.png)


## Introduction

This dashboard was designed for **job seekers, career switchers, and professionals exploring new opportunities** who often struggle to understand the rapidly changing data job market due to scattered information sources. By using a real-world dataset of 2024 data science job postings — including job titles, salaries, and locations — the project brings everything together into a single interactive platform that makes it easier to analyze market trends, compare compensation, and explore career opportunities.

## Skills Demonstrated

This project highlights a wide range of Power BI and data analysis skills used to transform raw job market data into meaningful business insights.

- **⚙️ Data Transformation with Power Query (ETL):** Cleaned and prepared raw datasets by handling missing values, correcting data types, and creating calculated columns for analysis.

- **🧮 Data Modeling & Measures:** Built key metrics and KPIs such as `Median Yearly Salary` and `Job Count` using Power BI calculations and measures.

- **📊 Data Visualization:** Created insightful visualizations using **Bar Charts, Column Charts, Line Charts,** and **Area Charts** to analyze hiring trends, salary patterns, and job distributions.

- **🗺️ Geospatial Analysis:** Used **Map Visualizations** to explore and display the global spread of data-related job opportunities.

- **🔢 KPI Cards & Detailed Tables:** Designed interactive KPI cards for quick insights and sortable tables for deeper data exploration.

- **🎨 Dashboard Design & Storytelling:** Developed a clean, user-friendly, and visually engaging dashboard layout focused on improving readability and delivering clear data-driven stories.

- **🖱️ Interactive Features:**
  - **Slicers:** Enabled dynamic filtering by job title and other categories.
  - **Buttons & Bookmarks:** Created smooth and intuitive page navigation.
  - **Drill-Through Functionality:** Allowed users to move from summary-level insights to detailed contextual analysis.
